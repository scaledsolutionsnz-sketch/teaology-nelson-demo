Our story

A little bubble tea shop with a big tea obsession

Teaology started with a simple idea: bubble tea that tastes like real tea, not just sugar. We brew our bases fresh, cook our pearls through the day and shake every cup to order at our spot on Hardy Street in the middle of Nelson.

Whether you're after the nostalgia of a classic pearl milk tea, a bright fruit tea on a sunny walk through town, or a matcha to power the afternoon — we'll build it exactly how you like it.

  • Made fresh to orderBrewed bases, real fruit and pearls cooked through the day — no shortcuts.
  • Customise everythingSweetness, ice, toppings and dairy or non-dairy milk — your call on every drink.
  • Central & easyRight on Hardy Street for a quick takeaway or a sit-down catch-up in town.
