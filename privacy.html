1. Overview

Teaology Nelson respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the website collects and how it's used, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

This is a simple brochure website with no contact form. We don't collect names, emails or payment details through this site. If you choose to call or email us, we'll only use those details to respond to your enquiry.

  • Contact you start: if you phone or email us, we receive whatever you share.
  • Basic analytics: anonymous, aggregated visit data may be collected to understand how the site is used.
  • Third-party embeds: Google Maps and social links may set their own cookies under their own policies.

3. How we use it

Any information you send is used only to answer your question, take an order enquiry, or improve our service. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

4. Cookies

Embedded services such as Google Maps may use cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Turning them off won't stop you using the core parts of this site.

5. Third-party services

When you use delivery platforms, Google Maps or our social media pages, their own privacy policies apply. We recommend reviewing them.
